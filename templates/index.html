How this is kept honest

One entry per project, one dated source per claim, with filings and regulator notices preferred over trade press and aggregators never used. A validator runs before every build and refuses to publish a file with a mistyped status, a chronology that runs backwards, a reactor placed outside China, or an entry claiming verification while citing only a corporate homepage.

Status is tracked through the full lifecycle. Stalled and cancelled projects stay on the page, because announcements that never became concrete are the most useful part of the record, but their output is excluded from the generating total above.

A scheduled job sweeps Chinese and English sources every fortnight and flags anything that contradicts this file. It never edits the data. A person checks the primary source and makes the commit.
